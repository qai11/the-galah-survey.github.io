#### Scientific Motivation of GALAH 2

The GALAH project used the HERMES spectrograph with 400 fibres, R=28k, 4 bands 100nm, (See GALAH DR4 [Buder *et al.* (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19858v1)) we want to get higher precision abundance 0.02 dex (5%) dex measurements so a higher resolution is needed. By combining the HERMES data with the Veloce data we are able to obtain this high precison abudances and ages at high SNR >100-1000. With the Veloce data being at a much higher resolution R~80,000 we will be able to obtain isotope meausurements which is important for further understanding of nucleosynthetic processes.

With Veloce we are able to reach lower wavelengths down to 390nm. This is important because a lot of heavy neutron capture elements show spectral activity in the blue end of the spectrum < 480nm.

### Projects of GALAH 2

* The initial data set includes 200 stars (Gaia FGK Benchmark Stars [Jofre *et al.* (2015)](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2015A&A...582A..81J/abstract)), ([Soubiran *et al.* (2024)](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024A%26A...682A.145S/abstract)) observed by Veloce R ~ 80,000.
* High resolution is needed to break line degeneracies (e.g. blending of unique lines)
* High resolution needed for line asymmetries (e.g. isotope ratios)
* GALAH 2 will have multiple projects:
  - GALAH 2 Phase 1 (Hi-Fi Red Giants, Veloce; R=80,000, ~200 stars, SNR > 100, @ [Fe/H] = -0.8, -0.5 [1star/obs, G~7-9])
  - GALAH 2 Phase 2 (Bright Giants 2dF; R=28,000 ~6000 stars, SNR > 100, stars across -2 < [Fe/H] < 0.5, over narrow temperature range; [20-50 fibers/field G ~6-11])
  - GALAH 2 Phase 3 (GALAH-2 & Metal-Poor 2dF; R=28,000 ~60,000 stars SNR > 30, with ~GALAH survey selection + pre-selected [Fe/H] < -1.0 stars in each field [remaining fibers, G~10.7-14])
* The Elements that will be obtained include
  - α-elements: Mg, O, Si, Ti, Ca;  Iron-peak: Fe, Mn, Cr, Co, V, Cu, Zn; 
  - Light/odd-Z: C, N, Na, Al, K, Sc; Neutron-capture: Y, Ce, Ba, La, Sr, Rb, Zr, Pr,  Sm, Gd, Mo, Ru, Nd, Eu
  - Isotopes: 24Mg/25Mg/26Mg , 12C/13C, 135+137Ba/138Ba, 48Ca/40Ca
